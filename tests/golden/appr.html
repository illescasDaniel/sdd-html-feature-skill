Approvals

Record only approvals actually received in chat, with the date, the tab content approved (hash or short description) and the user’s words. A page control never approves a gate. Later edits to status labels or traceability are not re-approvals; a change to behaviour, scope or contracts reopens the gate that owns it. Hashes identify what was approved, not what the page shows today.
